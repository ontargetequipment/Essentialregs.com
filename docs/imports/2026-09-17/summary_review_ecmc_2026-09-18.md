# Summary review — ECMC rules (2 CCR 404-1) — Sept 18, 2026

3,257 summaries reviewed by 34 Sonnet reviewer agents in two waves (~96 rows each, full text read, parent chapeau supplied). 3,144 approved as generated; 113 corrected (original kept in `summary_original`). First-pass rate 96.5%.

Recurring failure modes: attributing approvals, notifications and consultations to "the Commission" where the text names the Director or nobody (by far the largest group); invented acronym expansions (OFV = Order Finding Violation, AOC = Administrative Order by Consent, FV = Fired Vessel, SHWC = Solid and Hazardous Waste Commission); describing cross-referenced rules from memory; editorial rationale; may/will reversals; narrowing "operations regulated by the Commission" to oil and gas in the 100 Series definitions (the Act also covers geothermal and geologic storage). Rule 1304 (geothermal consultation) alone had 13 — the Operator/Director-vs-Commission confusion concentrated there.
