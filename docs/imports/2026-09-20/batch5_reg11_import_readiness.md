## Anything the summarizer should be warned about for this regulation

- **Two agencies.** "Division" = the **Air Pollution Control Division** of CDPHE (definition II.24 — analyzer approval, technical specifications, program design, Appendix A). Licensing, enforcement, station/mechanic licenses, fines and the field program are the **Department of Revenue** ("Executive Director of the Department of Revenue" / "Executive Director", definition II.30; Part D and Part E throughout; "DOR" in Attachment V). Never merge them; "the Department" in Parts D/E is Revenue, not CDPHE.
- **Program areas.** "Program Area" (II.53, by reference to § 42-4-304(20), C.R.S.), "enhanced" vs "basic" program areas, the "North Front Range Area" (II.49 — Larimer/Weld, a *state-only* program expressly not in the SIP), "Estes Park Area" (II.29) and the "Clean Screen Program" (II.15) are defined terms — scope every provision exactly as the text does; never generalize to statewide.
- **Emissions limits only as printed.** Part F's cutpoints are by model year and vehicle class (light-duty % CO / ppm HC; heavy-duty split at 1978/1979 and 6000/8500 lbs GVWR; grams/mile HC/CO/NOx transient limits; 5 % opacity; gas-cap 6 inches of water over 10 seconds / 60 cc/min at 30 inches; Clean Screen 0.50 % CO / 1,000 ppm NOx / 200 ppm HC). Point to the table; never invent, round or extrapolate a cutpoint or a model-year boundary. Part F III.A and III.B are "Repealed" — say so.
- **Acronyms** only as this regulation defines them: AIR (Automobile Inspection and Readjustment), OBD/OBD II, MIL, DLC, DTC, KOEO/KOER, TAS, IM 240, TSI (two-speed idle), BAR 84/90/97 (California Bureau of Automotive Repair specifications), Colorado 94 / Colorado 97 analyzers, CO-OBD-TAS, ECI, LDV/HDV, GVW(R), MSO/MCO, VIN, VIR, VID.
- **Part H (48 rows) is rulemaking history** (1996–2025) — describe what was adopted and when; never present it as a current requirement. Entries cite repealed structure (Part G's old pilot project, Part F III.A/B) that no longer exists.
- **Appendix A (174 rows) is analyzer technical specification for manufacturers** ("Revised Sept 09, 1994"; Attachment IV = Colorado 97, Attachment V = OBD stand-alone analyzer). It is not a motorist or station duty; Attachments I–III are incorporated by reference only; Attachment VI is images. **Appendix B is repealed (11/30/2014)** and its row also carries the Editor's Notes history.
- Dates to keep verbatim: Part G's High Emitter Program "Effective April 1, 2027"; Part A I.C schedules refer to statute (§§ 42-4-304(3)(b)(II), 42-4-310(1)(b)(II)) rather than stating the cycle — do not invent a cycle.
- Definition rows (61) each contain only their term's definition; a few define two terms in one row (II.5 BAR 90 / BAR 97; II.20 OBD TAS; II.30 Executive Director).
