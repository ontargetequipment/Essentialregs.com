## Error modes worth remembering (feed the hints on the next 49 CFR batch)
- **RMV.** Defined once per part as *rupture-mitigation valve*, but the summarizer expanded it as "remote mainline valve", "remote or manual valve", "remote or automatic shut-off valve" — 3 rows in Part 192, 10 in Part 195. Add "RMV = rupture-mitigation valve; RCV = remote-control valve; ASV = automatic shut-off valve" to the p192/p195 hints.
- **Invented repair-schedule categories.** Four § 192.933 rows cite a "180-day" category that does not exist there (the 180 days is § 192.933(b)'s discovery period); one § 195.452(h) row called the 60-day category "one-year".
- **Describing a cross-reference from memory** (§ 192.605(e), § 195.589(c) — which got § 195.579's subject backwards, § 199.103(b)(2), § 194.119(d)).
- **Scope drift**: "covered segment" narrowed to "high consequence area" (§ 192.939(b)); "downstream" reversed to "upstream" (§ 195.1(b)(10)(i)); a six-month grace period extended to existing segments (§ 192.620(d)).
- **"Not shown" claims about content that is shown** (§ 192.3 wrinkle bend, § 195.106(a) design formula) — the model sometimes assumes a formula is an image.
- **Invented examples** ("such as taps or branch connections", "such as a school, hospital, or environmental resource").
- No PHMSA/EPA confusion, no Colorado leakage, no wrong dates or thresholds in either batch — the 49 CFR hints held.
