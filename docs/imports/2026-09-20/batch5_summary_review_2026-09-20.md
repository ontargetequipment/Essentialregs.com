## Overruled flags

## Failure modes seen
- **Agency confusion (Reg 11)** — the largest cluster: summaries assigning licensing, suspension and revocation to "the Division" (APCD) when Reg 11 Parts D/E give those to the Department of Revenue, and equating the Commission with the Division. The hint names both agencies; the summarizer still defaulted to the Division.
- **Inserting a figure defined elsewhere in the regulation (Reg 27)** — six rows had "$89/mt CO2e" inserted where the text says only "the 2030 social cost of GHGs" (and the defined figure is per ton of CO2, not CO2e). Also GHG BAECT expanded wrongly.
- **Invented acronym expansions (Reg 11)** — TAS as "Technical Assistance System" / "transient analyzer system" / "test and service system" (it is Test Analyzer System), DIS/OEM, CO-OBD-TAS.
- **Options read as mandates (Reg 25)** — control-device alternatives under "one of the following" or rows ending "; or" summarized as fixed requirements (5 rows); invented geography (4 rows).
- **Shall/may and edge conditions** — "may consider" vs criteria, "below" vs "does not exceed", "whichever is longer", "at least until June 15" vs "by no later than June 15".
- **Cross-references described from memory**, definitions rewritten as duties, and one stray generation artifact ("Human" appended to `sec-12-A-II-A-2-a`).
