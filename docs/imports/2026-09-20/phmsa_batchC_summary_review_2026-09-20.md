## Corrections (id — reason)
- `sec-p190-190.208-(c)` — Summary says 'Within 30 days of receiving a notice of probable violation, the respondent may request a copy of the violation report', but the row's text puts no 30-day limit on the request — it says the request may be made 'Before or after responding in accordance with paragraph (a) ... or (b)'; the 30-day deadline in the chapeau governs the answer, not the report request.
- `sec-p190-190.213-(a)-(2)` — Summary says the penalty amount and payment procedures are stated 'in the recommended decision'/'the decision', but the chapeau says 'The final order includes—' these items; the final order, not the recommended decision, is the instrument.
- `sec-p190-190.223-(b)` — Summary describes the cross-reference from memory — '(a provision of the Clean Water Act dealing with pipeline facilities)' — but the text only cites '33 U.S.C. 1321(j)' without describing it.
- `sec-p190-190.233-(e)-(1)` — Summary says the Associate Administrator 'may consider' this factor, but the chapeau says the Associate Administrator 'shall consider, if relevant' — a mandatory duty (when relevant) read as discretionary.
- `sec-p190-190.233-(e)-(2)` — Summary says the Associate Administrator 'may consider' this factor, but the chapeau says the Associate Administrator 'shall consider, if relevant' — a mandatory duty (when relevant) read as discretionary.
- `sec-p190-190.233-(f)-(6)` — Summary says 'If the operator waived the chance for a hearing', but under paragraph (b) it is the Associate Administrator who waives the notice-and-hearing requirement; the text says only 'If the opportunity for a hearing was waived pursuant to paragraph (b)'.
- `sec-p190-190.236-(a)` — Summary states the order 'will contain a written description of the hazard and the required actions', but the text ends at 'a written description of:' and the listed items are not in this row.
- `sec-p190-190.237-(j)-(2)` — Summary says the decision is due 'within 30 days of receiving it' (the petition for reconsideration), but the text says 'no later than 30 days after receipt of the petition for review'.
- `sec-p190-190.239-(a)` — Summary says 'after notice and a hearing', but the text says 'after notice and an opportunity for hearing under paragraph (b)' – a hearing is not required to have occurred.
- `sec-p190-190.291-(d)` — Summary says 'It is a criminal offense if you willfully and knowingly excavate without first calling the one-call system ... or ... without paying attention to the ... markings', presenting the chapeau as a complete offense, but the text is a fragment ending in 'and' — the excavation conduct is only the first element and is criminal only when combined with the subsequent damage described in the following paragraphs.
- `sec-p190-190.291-(d)-(2)` — Summary says 'digs without calling 811 (or the local one-call system)' — the text says only 'an available one-call notification system'; '811' appears nowhere in the text; the summary also says the person is 'subject to criminal penalties under this part' when this paragraph (ending 'or') states no penalty itself.
- `sec-p190-190.291-(d)-(3)` — Summary says 'digs without calling 811' — the text says only 'an available one-call notification system'; '811' appears nowhere in the text.
- `sec-p190-190.341-(f)-(1)-(v)-(F)` — Summary adds the condition 'or the latest ILI survey result if more recent'; the text says only 'during the previous five years or latest ILI survey result' with no 'if more recent' qualifier.
- `sec-p190-190.341-(m)` — Summary describes subpart B's content ("such as a notice of probable violation, compliance order, or civil penalty") when the text only cites "the enforcement actions under subpart B of this part".
- `sec-p193-193.2321-(d)` — Summary narrows the re-examination duty to inspections 'performed since the last valid calibration', a limit the text does not state — the text requires re-examination of 'all previous weld inspections that are suspect'.
- `sec-p193-193.2511-(b)` — Summary narrows "a burning pool of impounded liquid" to "a burning pool of spilled LNG"; the text does not limit the impounded liquid to LNG.
- `sec-p193-193.2513-(c)-(5)` — Summary reads the condition wrong: it says loading is allowed only if the vehicle is "in exclusive LNG service and contains a positive pressure" or is in exclusive service without positive pressure and has been tested/purged, which leaves a vehicle "not in exclusive LNG service" barred entirely, whereas the text lets both a non-exclusive-service vehicle and an exclusive-service vehicle lacking positive pressure be loaded once the oxygen content is tested and, if over 2 percent by volume, purged.
- `sec-p193-193.2629-(a)-(2)-(ii)` — Summary invents scope not in the text or chapeau: "This applies to LNG facility operators using cathodic protection to control external corrosion on buried or submerged components" — the row text says only "A cathodic protection system designed to protect components in their entirety…" and the chapeau is "The following means:"; nothing in the row mentions external corrosion or buried/submerged components.
- `sec-p193-193.2707-(b)` — Summary describes the cross-referenced paragraph's content from memory: "hasn't completed the required operations and maintenance training (from paragraph (a))" and "someone who has met the training requirements" — the text says only "does not meet the requirements of paragraph (a) of this section" and does not say what those requirements are.
- `sec-p196-196.201` — Summary adds 'The enforcement process itself is governed by 49 CFR Part 190', but the row's text never mentions Part 190; it says this subpart itself 'prescribes the procedures governing the exercise of that authority and the imposition of those sanctions', and it names the Associate Administrator for Pipeline Safety, not PHMSA generally, as the one exercising the authority.
