## Anything the summarizer should be warned about for this regulation
- **GEMM 2 facility names and targets only as printed.** The 18 covered facilities and their higher-of-2021/2022 emissions, baselines (TABLE 1) and per-facility 2024–2029 / 2030 reduction percentages (TABLE 2) exist ONLY in the October 2023 statement of basis (`sec-27-E-IV`), "as of the date of rule adoption" and "subject to change to correct prior data or reporting inaccuracies". The operative rule (Part B, Tables 1–5) sets requirements by TIER (percent reduction achieved since 2015; percent contribution to the group), never by name — a summary of a Part B row must not name facilities or invent tonnages, and a summary of entry IV must quote the numbers exactly (e.g. Suncor 951,898; Western Sugar baseline 109,141 vs 81,981) or not at all.
- **Defined terms (Part A Section II — use as defined).** "GHG credit" (one metric ton CO2e, vintage-year specific, subject to Part D's expiration/double-counting rules); "GEMM 2 facility" and "GEMM 2 facility GHG baseline emissions"; "2015 GHG emissions" (as reported to EPA's GHGRP for calendar year 2015); "EITE stationary source" (energy-intensive trade-exposed — a defined class, not every manufacturer); "GHG BAECT" (best available emission control technology — Reg 27's own term, not Reg 3 BACT); "energy BMP"; "Non-GHG BAECT emissions"; "annual emissions limitation"; "audit" / "audit plan" / "audit scope" / "audit team" / "qualified third-party auditor"; "auction administrator" and "auction settlement price". "Division" = APCD; "Commission" = AQCC; "Part 98" = 40 CFR Part 98 (EPA GHGRP).
- **Numbers as printed, never converted or remembered.** The 2030 social cost of GHGs is fixed by definition (II.B.: $89/mt CO2, $2,500/mt CH4, $33,000/mt N2O); the applicability threshold is 25,000 metric tons CO2e/yr direct emissions (once in, stays in — Part A I.B.); EITE reduction is 5 % (the "0.95" in the Part C III.A.1. formula); Table 5 tiers are 6/5/4/3 %; the CHP compliance credit is capped at 50 % and sunsets December 31, 2029.
- **Dates and deadlines as printed.** Auctions: notice the third Tuesday of May, intent to participate by May 31, auction June 30 "or first business day thereafter" beginning 2025 (Part D IV); GHG reduction plans due September 30, 2025 (glass container manufacturers "no later than June 1, 2027"); annual emission reports March 31 starting 2025, compliance certifications September 30 starting 2027; GHG crediting and tracking system established by December 1, 2024. Compliance years 2024–2029 vs "2030 and beyond" are distinct regimes throughout Part B.
- **Statement-of-basis rows are history, not rules.** Entries I and II describe the pre-April-2023 Reg 22 Part B layout and cite section numbers that no longer exist; entry IV explains the reorganisation ("fka Part B, Sections III. through IX."). A summary must not present those citations as current Reg 27 sections. The CHP six-step formula in entry IV is the Division-approved-form method explained in the basis, not rule text.
- **Two regulations are intertwined.** Reporting is "pursuant to Regulation Number 22, Part A"; midstream/utility credit trading provisions in Part D refer to Regulation Number 7, Part B, Section VII. — keep the source regulation named when a Reg 27 row merely points to it.
