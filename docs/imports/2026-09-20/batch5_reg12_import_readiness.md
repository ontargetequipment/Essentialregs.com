## Anything the summarizer should be warned about (also baked into `REG_PROMPT_HINTS["12"]`)
- **Two separate programs**: Part A = Diesel Fleet Self-Certification Program (fleets of nine or more diesel vehicles > 14,000 lb GVWR, self-inspected by the fleet's certified inspectors; State-Only, not SIP); Part B = Diesel Opacity Inspection Program (Department-of-Revenue-licensed stations inspecting individual vehicles). Never merge their requirements, fees, or inspector qualifications.
- **"Division"** = Air Pollution Control Division of CDPHE (Part A I.B.6.); **"Department"** in Part B = the Colorado Department of Revenue (Part B I.B.15.), which licenses stations and inspectors — not CDPHE. "Commission" = the AQCC.
- **"Fleet" / "fleet owner"** only as Part A I.B.8. defines them (nine or more diesel vehicles over 14,000 lb GVWR registered/routinely operated in the program area or principally operated from a terminal there); "Excessive Violation" (I.B.7.), "program area" (42-4-401(8) C.R.S. — never enumerate counties), "Certification of Emissions Control (CEC)" only as defined.
- **Opacity percentages and averaging windows exactly as printed** — "twenty percent (20%) opacity measured over five (5) seconds" (Part C I), "over 5 seconds" (Part A VII), and Part B V.A's "over five (5)" with the missing word; never invent a cutpoint, model-year table or unit conversion. Model-year cutoffs verbatim: "1991 and later model year" tampering inspections (A IV.D.4., B III.A.4.), "model year 2014 or newer … twenty six thousand pounds" exemptions (A I.D.4., B I.D.2.b.iii.), "fourth"/"sixth model year", "ten model years old" test-cycle splits.
- **Test steps are numbered procedures** (A IV.C.1.-3., B III.B./III.C.) with "Step 1.c."-style internal references and one "Reserved" step (III.C.4.b.vi.) — a placeholder, not a gap; opacity-meter/dynamometer specs (B II.C., II.D.) are equipment criteria — quote ranges/tolerances as printed.
- **SAE J1667** and **40 C.F.R. Part 85, Subpart V (January 24, 2023)** are incorporated by name — name them, never describe their contents.
- **Part D** (I-XI) is rulemaking history (1998-2025), not current requirements; entry XI's repeated "There are no federal requirements…" is per-factor boilerplate. The I.D.7.d.ii. list has no "E." — do not "correct" it.
