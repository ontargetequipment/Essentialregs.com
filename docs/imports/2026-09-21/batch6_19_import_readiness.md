## Anything the summarizer should be warned about
- **"Division" is the APCD, not "the Department"**; this is CDPHE/APCD's lead program (delegated from EPA under 40 CFR Part 745), not an air-emission rule — do not describe abatement as emission control or "the Department" as the regulator.
- Two parallel definition sets: Part A II. (84 terms) and Part B II. (6 terms + "definitions in Part A apply"); "renovation" is *excluded* from Part A (I.D.: not abatement) and *is* Part B's subject.
- Numbers to quote verbatim, never converted: lead-based paint = more than 0.06 % by wet weight / 0.5 % by dry weight / 1 mg/cm² (II.B.47.); elevated blood lead level ≥ 5 µg/dL (II.B.31.); dust-lead hazard 10 µg/ft² floors / 100 µg/ft² interior sills, soil 400 / 1,200 µg/g (II.B.49.); clearance levels 10 / 100 / 400 / 500 / 800 µg/ft² (II.B.14., IV.H.3.a., V.J.1.g.); course lengths and accreditation fees (III.A.2.f., III.A.6.e.: "Inspector (24-hour) course --$1,500", "Risk Assessor (16-hour) course -- $1,000", "Supervisor (32-hour) course -- $2,000" …); certification fees "$180 for each year of certification sought" (III.B.5.b.), firm fees "$600" (III.B.6.b.(i)(A)); the V.A.5.c. notification fee bands ("$145 base plus $8.00 per $1,000 in valuation or fraction thereof"); re-certification every 3 or 5 years (III.B.5.a.); the 10-working-day notification (V.A.5.a.); the 45-day variance decision (V.M.); Appendix A row values — read the band, do not interpolate between rows (and mind the two printed typos).
- III.B.4. is "(Reserved)" (restored by the label fix) — a summary must say it is reserved/removed, not invent "certification based on prior training".
- Part B V. sample forms are *samples* ("The following is a sample of language that could be used") — not mandatory text; the underscore lines are signature blanks.
- Part C entries are rulemaking history; entry V re-states the outline under "PART A." / "PART B." headings and cites old/mistyped numbering ("II.B.48.b"); entries I–IV describe the 1998–2007 rule text, not the current one.
- V.C.2.a. warning-sign wording (WARNING / LEAD ABATEMENT WORK AREA / HAZARDOUS LEAD DUST / POISON / DO NOT ENTER) is verbatim mandatory sign text.
- Acronyms as defined: LAF/LEF (Certified Lead Abatement / Evaluation Firm), EBL (elevated blood lead level), HUD, TSCA, OPP (occupant protection plan, Part C only); "target housing" and "child-occupied facility" are terms of art (pre-1978 housing with exceptions; a building visited regularly by the same child under six) — never paraphrase them loosely.
