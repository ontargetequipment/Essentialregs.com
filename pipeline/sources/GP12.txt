I.    General Permit Applicability

I.A   This general permit may be used only for oil and gas well production facilities as
      defined in Regulation Number 7, Part B, Section I.B.33 and Section II.A.46 (Adopted:
      04/18/2025, Effective: 06/14/2025). Equipment covered by this general permit is
      limited to the following sources:

      I.A.1     Natural gas-fired reciprocating internal combustion engines.

      I.A.2     Diesel fuel-fired reciprocating internal combustion engines.

      I.A.3     Storage tanks used to store condensate, crude oil, intermediate
                hydrocarbon liquids, or produced water:

                I.A.3.a   Condensate storage tanks with a design capacity less than or
                          equal to 10,000 barrels per storage vessel, used for the storage of
                          condensate.

                I.A.3.b   Crude oil storage tanks with a design capacity less than or equal
                          to 10,000 barrels per storage vessel, used for the storage of crude
                          oil.

                I.A.3.c   Intermediate hydrocarbon liquid storage tanks with a design
                          capacity less than or equal to 10,000 barrels per storage vessel,
                          used for storage of intermediate hydrocarbon liquid.

                I.A.3.d   Produced water storage tanks used for the storage of produced
                          water.

      I.A.4     Hydrocarbon liquid loading operations including condensate loading, crude
                oil, and intermediate hydrocarbon liquid loading.

II.    General Operating Conditions

II.A   The owner or operator must notify the Division no later than fifteen (15) days after
       commencement of operation of an emissions unit under this general permit by
       submitting a Notice of Startup (NOS) to the Division. The NOS must be submitted using
       the Division’s online submission tool on the Division’s self-certification webpage.
       Individual NOSs must be submitted per emissions unit. Failure to notify the Division of
       startup of the permitted source is a violation of Regulation Number 3, Part B, Section
       III.G.1. and can result in the revocation of the permit.

II.B   The general permit registration expires if the owner or operator of the sources for
       which this general permit was issued: (i) does not commence construction,
       modification or operation of these sources within 18 months after either the date of
       completeness of the general permit registration or the date on which such
       construction or activity was scheduled to commence as set forth in the permit
       application associated with this general permit; (ii) discontinues construction for a
       period of eighteen months or more; (iii) does not complete construction within a
       reasonable time of the estimated completion date. The Division may grant extensions
       of the deadline. (Regulation Number 3, Part B, Section III.F.4.)

II.C   The operator must complete all initial compliance testing and sampling as required in
       this general permit.

II.D    Air pollution control equipment may be used to comply with the emission limits and
        performance standards required in this general permit. (Regulation Number 7, Part B,
        Section I.B.2.)

II.E    This general permit may not be used to grant an overall control efficiency from
        enclosed combustion devices over 95% for Volatile Organic Compounds (VOC).

II.F    The owner or operator must construct and operate the facility in accordance with the
        general permit registration approval letter, or if an approval letter has not been
        issued yet, as certified by the owner or operator and/or required in the approved
        GP12 modeling workbook, the approved APCD Form 114, or the approved ambient air
        impact analysis (whichever is applicable). This includes, but is not limited to, stack
        heights, stack diameters, source’s location relative to the ambient air boundary,
        emission rates, and preclusion of public access to the site.

III.    Annual Emission Limitation

III.A   Equipment Specific Emission Limitations

        III.A.1   The maximum allowable VOC, NOx, and CO emissions from each emission
                  point registered under this general permit must not exceed the requested
                  annual emission limitations (tons per year) as listed in the general permit
                  registration approval letter, or if an approval letter has not been issued, as
                  certified by the owner or operator in the Air Pollutant Emission Notices
                  (APEN) included with the general permit registration. (Regulation Number 3,
                  Part B, Sections II.A.4. and II.A.7.)

        III.A.2   The owner or operator must operate and maintain the equipment registered
                  under the general permit with the air pollution control equipment as listed
                  in the general permit registration together with the process limitations in



                                          Page 11 of 107
                  Section IV to reduce emissions to less than or equal to the emissions limits
                  established in this general permit. (Regulation Number 3, Part B, Sections
                  II.A.4. and II.A.7.)

III.B   Facility-Wide Emission Limitations

        III.B.1   The maximum allowable emissions of hazardous air pollutants (HAPs) from
                  all permitted emission points at the stationary source must not exceed the
                  following limitations. (Regulation Number 3, Part B, Sections II.A.4. and
                  II.A.7.)

                  III.B.1.a Each Individual HAP:                     8.0 tons per year

                  III.B.1.b Combined total of all HAPs:              20.0 tons per year

III.C   For sources located at true minor facilities, compliance with the annual emission
        limitations in the Equipment Specific Emission Limitations and Facility-Wide Emission
        Limitations conditions above for both criteria and hazardous air pollutants must be
        determined on an annual calendar-year basis, unless required elsewhere in this
        general permit. The permit holder must keep a compliance record on-site or at a local
        field office for Division review.

III.D   For sources located at synthetic minor or major source subject to Condition I.F,
        compliance with the Equipment Specific Emission Limitations and Facility-Wide
        Emission Limitations for both criteria and hazardous air pollutants must be
        determined on a rolling twelve (12) month total basis. By the end of each month, a
        new twelve-month total is calculated based on the previous twelve months’ data.
        During the first year of operation, compliance must be based upon cumulative
        emissions from all months of operation. The permit holder must calculate actual

        for Division review.

6. Specific Provisions for Permanent Replacements

   6.1. Except as provided in Condition 5, the owner or operator may permanently
         replace an existing engine with another engine with the same manufacturer,

         requirements applicable to the existing engine, unless otherwise superseded by
         the regulations identified in the Applicability Report, as well as any other
         applicable requirements for the replacement engine. Measurement of emissions
         from the permanent replacement engine and compliance with the applicable
         emission limitations must be made as set forth in Condition 8 below.

8.4. The owner or operator must measure nitrogen oxide (NOx) and carbon
     monoxide (CO) emissions in the exhaust from the replacement engine using a
     portable flue gas analyzer within seven (7) calendar days of commencing

8.5. All portable analyzer monitoring required by this general permit must be
     conducted using the Division’s Portable Analyzer Monitoring Protocol (ver March
     2006 or newer) as found on the Division’s web site at:
     https://cdphe.colorado.gov/compliance-and-enforcement

8.6. Results of the portable analyzer monitoring must be used to monitor the
     compliance status of this unit, except for any specifically applicable emission
     limitations or standards for which an applicable requirement specifies a
     performance test or Continuous Emission Monitoring System as the compliance
     monitoring method.

8.7. For comparison with an annual (tons/year) or short term (lbs/unit of time)
     emission limit, the results of the monitoring event must be converted to a
     lb/hr basis and multiplied by the allowable operating hours in the month or
     year (whichever applies) in order to monitor compliance. If a source is not
     limited in its hours of operation the test results will be multiplied by the
     maximum number of hours in the month (744) or year (8760), whichever
     applies.

8.8. For comparison with a short-term limit that is either input based (lb/mmBtu),
     output based (g/hp-hr) or concentration based (ppmvd @ 15% O2) that the

     existing unit is currently subject to or the replacement engine will be subject
     to, the results of the test must be converted to the appropriate units as
     described in the above-mentioned Portable Analyzer Monitoring Protocol
     document.

8.9. If the portable analyzer results indicate compliance with both the NOx and CO
     emission limitations, in the absence of credible evidence to the contrary, the
     owner or operator may certify that the engine complies with both the NOx and
     CO emission limitations for the relevant time period.

8.10. Subject to the provisions of § 25-7-123.1, C.R.S. and in the absence of credible
     evidence to the contrary, if the portable analyzer results fail to demonstrate
     compliance with either the NOx or CO emission limitations, the engine will be
     considered to be out of compliance from the date of the portable analyzer test
     until a portable analyzer test indicates compliance with both the NOx and CO
     emission limitations or until the engine is taken offline.
